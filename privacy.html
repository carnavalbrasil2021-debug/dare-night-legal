Dare Night — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Dare Night ("the app") is published by FG GLOBAL ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Player names you type and your game settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads may use a device identifier if you allow it.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to confirm which decks you own.

Information stored on your device

Player names, chosen decks, temporary deck unlocks and game counters are saved locally on your device so the app remembers them next time. You can delete them at any time in Settings > "Clear players and settings", or by deleting the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, we show ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, the IDFA, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks; on Android, the Android advertising ID), IP address (used for approximate location), device type, and interactions with ads, to serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. If you do not allow tracking (iPhone) or you delete or reset your advertising ID (Android), ads are still shown but are not personalized using the advertising identifier. Users in regions that require it (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland) are asked for consent and can change their choice at any time in Settings > "Ad privacy choices".

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

If you buy "All Access" or "Remove ads", no ads are shown.

Purchases (Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat)

All payments are handled by Apple through your Apple ID (iPhone) or by Google Play through your Google account (Android); we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase history for this app and basic device information. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • We do not store the content of your games on any server.

Age

Dare Night is rated 18+ on the App Store, is intended for adults (18+) on Google Play, and is not directed to children under 13. Some decks are marked 18+ and ask players to confirm their age before use.

Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. For data processed by Google or RevenueCat, you can use the controls described in their policies, reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings or in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads), or contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
